# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The
expectations and enforcement process below apply in addition to that text.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include showing
empathy, respecting different viewpoints and experiences, giving and accepting
constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what
is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
language or attention, trolling, sustained disruption, deliberate outing or
publication of private information, and other conduct that a reasonable person
would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
comments, project-run chat or events, and when someone officially represents the
project in public. It also applies to private interactions that directly affect
participation in this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not use a public issue to describe a conduct incident or identify the people
involved. Do not use the security-advisory channel; it is reserved for software
vulnerabilities.

The project has not yet published a dedicated private conduct address. Until one
is listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md):

- For conduct on GitHub, use GitHub's private **Report abuse or spam** control on
  the relevant content or account.
- If project-maintainer follow-up is also needed, open a minimal issue titled
  `Confidential conduct contact requested`. Include no incident details,
  screenshots, names, or other identifying information. A non-conflicted
  maintainer will arrange a private channel acceptable to the reporter.

If every project maintainer is involved in the report, use GitHub's reporting
mechanism and do not send incident details to those maintainers. The project
must designate an independent responder before it can investigate on the
community's behalf.

Maintainers receiving a report must protect the reporter's privacy as far as
possible, disclose information only to people needed to investigate and respond,
and recuse themselves when they are involved or have a conflict of interest. No
one may retaliate against a good-faith reporter or witness.

After reviewing available evidence, maintainers may take actions ranging from a
private correction or warning through temporary exclusion to a permanent ban,
following the Contributor Covenant's Community Impact Guidelines. Decisions and
appeals should be documented privately; any public statement must omit details
that could identify a reporter without consent.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy.
They may remove or reject comments, commits, code, edits, issues, and other
contributions that violate it.
