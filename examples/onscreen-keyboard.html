⌨️ On-screen keyboard — the classic TV search pattern

A contained QWERTY grid drives a live search. ←→ wrap at the row edges, and focus never falls out of the keyboard by geometry — its only doors are explicit routes: ↑ from the top row and ↓ from the bottom row jump to the results rail, ↑ from a result lands back on the wide SPACE key (one stop, reachable from every key above it). A short Enter on ⌫ deletes one character; holding it ≥ 600 ms clears the query.
