🛠 Config console — a dense settings screen

Category rail on the left, field panel on the right: the classic spatial split. Native inputs keep their own arrow keys — ←→ move the slider and the text caret, Esc hands focus back to the rail. Data is MSW over fetch, cached by TanStack Query; the form is TanStack Form, and an SSH port outside 1–65535 comes back as a real 422 that lands on the offending field.
