📺 EPG / TV guide — a 2D virtualized time × channel grid

The hardest spatial-navigation shape there is: blocks are as wide as they are long, so rows never line up. Pressing ↓ from a two-hour film lands on whatever is actually below that point in time, not on the next channel's midnight programme. Channel rows are windowed with @tanstack/react-virtual and bridged with attachVirtualEdges; data comes from MSW via TanStack Query. ←↑↓→ moves, Enter records.
